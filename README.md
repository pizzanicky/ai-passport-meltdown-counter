# Meltdown Counter

## Introduction

Only you know the moments you break down each day on the grind.

How often does something break your focus? How often does someone cut you off mid-thought? The anger spikes, and there's nowhere for the pressure to go.

When you're interrupted, cut off, or about to snap, hit the confirm button. Let a little pressure out, and keep a count of how many times you broke down today.

Today shows today's count. Stats shows this week and the change since yesterday. The calendar uses color to show how heavy each day was.

Sound is on by default. Press and hold the confirm button to mute it.

The code is open source. You can build your own pressure-release tool.

## Instructions

Press the confirm button once to log a breakdown. The count updates and a sound plays. Up shows this week and the change since yesterday. Down shows this month. Press and hold confirm to mute. Muting does not stop the count. The screen sleeps after one minute of no input.

If the first-install date is not set, open the mini program "蓝牙配网-FoloToy AI PASSPORT" on your phone, select `BLUFI_FoloPassport`, and enter a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network to finish Bluetooth setup. Once the time syncs, it's ready to use.
